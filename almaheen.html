"Seek knowledge from the cradle to the grave" — a guiding principle behind every class we teach.

Al-Maheen Academy is an online Madrasah dedicated to helping Muslims around the world strengthen their relationship with Allah through authentic Islamic education.

We teach the Qur'an, Hadith, Arabic and Islamic manners over live classes on Google Meet and Zoom — with private plans for individuals and families, so learning fits around real life rather than the other way around.

Our aim is simple: make Islamic knowledge accessible, practical and steady, so every student — beginner or advanced, child or adult — can build a lasting connection to the Qur'an and Sunnah.

Our Mission

Make learning Islam simple, practical and accessible to everyone, regardless of age or location.

Our Vision

To become one of the leading international online Madrasahs, building a global community of knowledgeable Muslims.

What guides every class

Five principles shape how we teach, how we speak to students, and how we grow as an academy.

Authenticity

Every lesson is rooted in the Qur'an and authentic Sunnah.

Excellence

High-quality teaching delivered with genuine professionalism.

Consistency

Steady, sustainable progress rather than overwhelming students.

Sincerity

Seeking Allah's pleasure above all else, in teacher and student alike.

Accessibility

Islamic education made available to students worldwide.

What we teach

A steady curriculum covering the Qur'an, Arabic, Hadith and daily practice — taught one step at a time.

Qur'an Recitation & Tajweed

Read the Qur'an with correct articulation (makharij) and tajweed rules, building fluency verse by verse.

All ages

Qur'an Memorization (Hifdh)

Structured memorization starting from Juz Amma, with regular revision and personalised progress tracking.

All ages

Hadith Studies

Learn selected, authentic hadith with their meaning, context and how to apply them in daily life.

Ages 6–15 & Adults

Arabic Language

From letter recognition to reading and writing fluency, with a path toward Qur'anic vocabulary.

Ages 4–15 & Adults

Islamic Manners (Adab)

Everyday etiquette from the Sunnah — greeting, eating, the masjid, parents — through stories and practice.

Ages 4–12

Adhkar & Duas

Morning and evening remembrance, and supplications for daily moments — waking, eating, travelling and more.

All ages
